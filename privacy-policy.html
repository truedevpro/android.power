Android Power

Privacy Policy
Effective Date: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Android Power ("Android Power", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Android Power mobile application.

Android Power is designed to provide battery monitoring, power management, device controls, floating power controls, and battery optimization features.

Privacy at a glance: Android Power primarily processes device and battery information locally on your device to provide its features. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Access

Depending on the features you choose to use, Android Power may access certain device information and system capabilities.

Battery and Device Information

The application may access information such as:

  • Battery percentage
  • Battery charging status
  • Battery temperature
  • Battery voltage
  • Battery health/status
  • Charging current or charging speed, where supported by the device
  • Available device memory information
  • Basic device/system status required for application features

This information is used to display battery and device statistics and provide the application's power-management features.

2. Display Over Other Apps / Overlay Permission

Permission: Display over other apps

Android Power may request permission to display a floating power button or other power controls over other applications.

This permission allows the floating power button to remain accessible while you use other applications. The overlay is controlled by you and can be disabled at any time from your Android device settings.

Android Power does not use the overlay permission to read, capture, or collect the content displayed by other applications.

3. Background Operation

Android Power may operate in the background when you enable features that require continuous or periodic monitoring.

Background operation may be used for:

  • Maintaining the floating power button
  • Monitoring battery status
  • Updating battery information
  • Providing enabled power-related notifications
  • Keeping selected power-management features active

Android system controls may allow you to restrict background activity or stop the application at any time.

4. Foreground Service

If Android Power uses a foreground service for features such as the floating power button or continuous battery monitoring, Android displays the appropriate system notification as required by Android.

The foreground service is used only to provide the feature that you have enabled and does not give the application unrestricted access to other applications or their content.

5. Device Administrator Permission

Permission: Device Administrator

Android Power may request device administrator permission for supported device-control functionality, such as the Lock Screen feature.

This permission is only activated after you explicitly enable the relevant feature and approve the Android system prompt.

Android Power does not use device administrator privileges to access your personal files, messages, contacts, photographs, passwords, or other personal content.

You can revoke device administrator access from your Android device settings when the feature is no longer required.

6. Lock Screen and Power Controls

Android Power provides optional device-control features such as locking the screen and accessing power-related controls.

These features operate through Android's system APIs and permissions. The application does not obtain your account passwords, screen-lock PIN, pattern, biometric information, or other authentication credentials.

7. Notifications

Android Power may use notifications when required for enabled background or foreground features, such as battery monitoring or a floating power control service.

You can manage or disable notifications through your Android device's notification settings.

8. Advertising

Android Power may display advertisements through third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect or process certain information according to their own privacy policies. This may include advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location information, and information related to ad interaction, depending on your device settings, consent choices, and applicable laws.

Android Power does not sell your personal information to advertisers.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices through Google's privacy documentation.

9. Analytics and Crash Reporting

If analytics or crash-reporting services are enabled in the application, such services may collect technical information necessary to understand application performance and diagnose crashes.

This information may include application version, device model, Android version, crash information, and other technical diagnostics.

Such information is used to improve stability, performance, and reliability of Android Power.

10. Information We Do Not Collect

Android Power does not intentionally collect or access the following information unless a future feature explicitly requires it and appropriate permission is requested:

  • Contacts
  • SMS or call history
  • Photos or videos
  • Personal documents
  • Passwords or authentication credentials
  • Screen-lock PINs or patterns
  • Private content from other applications

11. Data Storage

Information required for Android Power's functionality may be processed and stored locally on your device.

We do not maintain a personal profile of users based solely on the battery and device information displayed by the application.

12. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers only when necessary for services used by the application, such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other technical services.

Such third-party providers process information according to their respective privacy policies and applicable laws.

13. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by Android Power. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Children's Privacy

Android Power is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

15. Your Choices

You remain in control of permissions granted to Android Power. Depending on your Android version and device manufacturer, you can manage permissions through system settings.

You may disable:

  • Display over other apps
  • Background activity
  • Notifications
  • Device administrator access
  • Other optional permissions

Disabling certain permissions may prevent the associated application feature from working.

16. Third-Party Services

Android Power may use third-party services, including services provided by Google, for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other application functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
