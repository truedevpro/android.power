Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 11, 2026

Android Power ("Android Power", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information and permissions are handled when you use the Android Power application.

1. Information We Access

Android Power may access certain device information and system capabilities when you use specific features of the application. This may include battery status, charging status, device power information and other information made available by the Android operating system.

2. Display Over Other Apps

Android Power may request the "Display over other apps" permission to provide its floating power button. This permission allows the application to display the floating control above other applications.

The overlay is used only to provide the functionality requested by the user and is not used to read the content of other applications.

3. Floating Power Button

The floating power button allows users to access supported power-related actions from other screens. The feature operates only when the user enables the required permission.

4. Device Administrator Permission

Android Power may request device administrator permission for supported device actions such as locking the screen.

This permission is optional and is only used when the corresponding feature is enabled by the user.

5. Background Operation

Some Android Power features may require background operation or an Android foreground service to keep the floating power control available while the user is using other applications.

Background operation is used only to provide the application's enabled functionality.

6. Battery and Device Information

The application may access information provided by Android regarding battery level, charging state, battery temperature, voltage, battery health and other available power-related metrics.

This information is used to display power and battery details to the user.

7. Data Collection

Android Power does not intentionally collect or store personally identifiable information through the application's core power-control features.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information accessed by the application for its core functionality is not intentionally shared with third parties except where required to provide a service that you have enabled or where required by law.

9. Advertising

Android Power may use third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob, to display advertisements. These services may collect or use information according to their own privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Android Power is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
